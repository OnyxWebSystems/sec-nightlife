## Suggested marketing angles

### For Party-Goers

1. **Discovery** — "Find what's happening tonight"
2. **Tables** — "Book or join a table without the hassle"
3. **Community** — "Connect with friends and the nightlife scene"
4. **Exclusivity** — "Members only" (invite-led positioning)

### For Business Owners

1. **Growth** — "List your venue and reach party-goers across South Africa"
2. **Operations** — "Events, bookings, tables, and menus — all in one dashboard"
3. **Insights** — "Analytics and promotions to grow your nightlife business"
4. **Trust** — Compliance and venue verification built in
