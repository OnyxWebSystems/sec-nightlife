# Sec Nightlife — Service Delivery & Fulfillment Policy

**Effective:** April 2026  
**Business:** Sec Nightlife (SEC)  
**Platform:** https://secnightlife.com  
**Applies to:** All users, venues, hosts, and promoters

> **Note for Paystack review:** Sec Nightlife is a nightlife **services marketplace**. We do not sell or ship physical goods. This document serves as our **Shipping / Fulfillment Policy** equivalent.

---

## 1. Nature of services

SEC facilitates bookings and payments for:

- Event tickets and venue entry
- Table reservations and hosted tables at licensed venues
- Optional menu items ordered in connection with a table booking (fulfilled by the venue on-site)
- Venue promotion listings in the app (digital advertising)

All real-world services are **delivered by the venue or host** at the venue premises. SEC provides the technology platform only.

---

## 2. Digital fulfillment (immediate)

After a successful Paystack payment:

1. The transaction is verified by SEC’s backend (Paystack webhook + verification).
2. **Tickets:** Digital tickets and QR codes are issued instantly in the customer’s Profile.
3. **Tables:** Booking confirmation is recorded in the app; the venue and host receive booking details in their dashboards.
4. **Promotions:** Paid venue promotions go live in the app after payment confirmation.

No physical shipment is involved.

---

## 3. In-venue service delivery

- The customer attends the venue on the booked date/time.
- The venue is solely responsible for admission, table service, alcohol service (where licensed), security, and compliance with South African law.
- SEC does not control venue operations, stock, or on-site fulfillment.

Venue obligations are defined in the [Venue Compliance Charter](https://secnightlife.com/VenueComplianceCharter).

---

## 4. Delivery timelines

| Product type | Delivery |
|--------------|----------|
| Event tickets | Immediate (digital, in-app) |
| Table bookings | Confirmed at payment; service on event date at venue |
| Menu items on tables | Prepared and served by venue on-site |
| Promotions | Live in app after payment |

There are **no courier, postal, or third-party logistics** partners.

---

## 5. Failed or undelivered services

If a venue cancels an event, closes, or fails to honour a booking, refund handling is governed by our [Refund Policy](https://secnightlife.com/RefundPolicy). The venue bears primary responsibility; SEC provides in-app refund request tools and support facilitation.
